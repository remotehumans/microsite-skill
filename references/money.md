# Money: rates, IR35 and the client's budget (job / contract mode, UK)

Run `python3 scripts/rate_maths.py --rates <day rates> --days <billable days> [--fte <advertised salaries> --fraction <part-time share>]`. Present results to the provider privately. Never put rates or take-home on the page.

## What to explain

- **Inside vs outside IR35**: control, substitution and mutuality of obligation. In-office, directed, no-substitute work is likely inside. Plan for inside and treat outside as something to earn by reshaping the deal (defined deliverables, own methods and tools, flexibility, right to substitute).
- **Who decides**: medium and large clients issue a Status Determination Statement; for a small client, the provider's own company decides and carries the risk.
- **Routes**: fixed-term PAYE employee; inside IR35 via an umbrella (employer NI 15% above £5,000, 0.5% levy and the umbrella fee come out of the rate; since April 2026 agencies are liable for non-compliant umbrellas); inside via own Ltd (deemed payment); outside via own Ltd.
- **Pricing rule of thumb**: inside IR35, quote the top of the range or above, because roughly 13% goes on employer costs before tax.
- **Client's view**: advertised full-time-equivalent salary × part-time fraction, plus employer NI and a 3% pension, plus a typical 20% placement fee for permanent or fixed-term hires; contractor cost includes a typical 15–20% agency margin. Ask the recruiter whether the submitted rate is the pay rate or the charge rate.

Rate floors, targets and recruiter questions live in the active profile's `notes.md` (check the provider's memory for updates).
