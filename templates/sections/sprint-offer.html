Prepare

Before day one. We agree one area to go deep on, such as [[A FUNCTION OR TEAM OF THEIRS]], who we'll speak to, and the dates. Your team shares a few real examples: a normal day, an awkward one and an exception.

Map the work

Week 1. Work discovery interviews with you and the people who do the work: [[THEIR ROLES]]. We follow real examples, like [[A REAL JOB OF THEIRS]], and draw how the work moves today.

Match and design

Week 2. We spot the waits, repeats and handovers, then match each one to the simplest fix: a clearer process, a tool you already have, automation or AI. We draw the future process and check it with the people who own it.

Playback

Final week. We walk you through the findings live, agree the priorities, and you choose the next step: do it yourselves, do it with us, or do nothing yet.

What you keep

  • A view of the whole business across the five functions, from the directors' side
  • An inventory of the workflows in the agreed area, with who owns each one
  • Process maps of how the key work runs today, checked by the people who do it
  • Proposed future process maps: what people do, what software or AI does, and who checks it
  • A baseline and benefit estimates, built from numbers your team confirms
  • A roadmap in order, with owners and what has to happen first
  • A full report, a live playback and editable files
