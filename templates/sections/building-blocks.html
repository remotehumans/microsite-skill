Start small

Why this order

AI is only as good as what it knows about your business. Most of that lives in people's heads, inboxes and old files. Once it's captured and the processes are clear, every AI workflow you build stands on solid ground, and each new one gets easier.

Starting small keeps the risk low and the learning fast. The first working workflow becomes the model the rest of the business can see, trust and copy.
