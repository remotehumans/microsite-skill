Start with the people

Why this order

AI is only as good as what it knows about your business, and most of that lives in [[THEIR PEOPLE]]'s heads, inboxes and notes. So the sprint starts by talking to people, then writes down how the work really happens, before anyone looks for places to use AI.

Each floor stands on the one below. The interviews give the knowledge, the knowledge gives the workflows, the workflows give the maps, and the maps show exactly where AI would earn its place.

What you build afterwards, and who builds it, is your choice.
