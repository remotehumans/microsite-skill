Why this matters

Early images help win councils, partners and communities over. With AI, one approved CGI can become every season, every hour and a resident's-eye moment within a few hours. That frees your visualisers for the hero shots.

How it was made

Each variation started from a CGI on your website and was edited with AI image generation, told to keep every building, window and street exactly as designed and change only light, season or medium. Then each one was checked against the original by eye.
