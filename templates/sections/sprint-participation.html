[[ONE LINE: the people who know how their work really happens, and the few hours asked of each]]

The directors

Set the goal and the area to look at. A 60-minute kick-off, and the playback at the end.

[[PROCESS OWNERS, e.g. managers and head chefs]]

Own the workflows we map. A 60 to 90-minute interview each, then a short session to check the maps.

[[PEOPLE DOING THE WORK]]

Show us the work as it really happens, including the awkward days and the exceptions.

Briefed first

You tell the team what the sprint is for before it starts, so nobody's caught off guard.

Treated with care
