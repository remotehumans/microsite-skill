Start with an AI Design Sprint

Weeks 1–3. Before building anything, find out how work really happens.

Map

Work discovery interviews with each team: how [[A CORE JOB, e.g. a sale moves from valuation to completion]], which tools, where the hours go. What people know gets written down and documented.

Match

Match the work to what AI does well today, and rank each opportunity by how much it increases revenue, reduces costs or saves time, plus ease, data readiness and risk.

Make

Build the first quick wins with the people who own the work, inside the tools you already use.

Measure

Set a baseline first, then measure hours saved, turnaround and quality, so every result has a number behind it.

Your data gets its own look

AI is only as good as what it can reach. Part of the sprint is understanding where [[THEIR KEY INFORMATION, e.g. listings, client files, policies and correspondence]] live, who can access what, and how tidy it is. If it's scattered, sorting that out becomes an early workstream, because it pays back in everything that follows.

What you have at the end of week 3

  • Simple process maps of how the key work flows
  • A data and tools readiness view
  • A ranked list of AI opportunities
  • A first team to start with, plus 2–3 quick wins already in use

How I work

Three parts, in this order: the people, the business, then the build.

1People first

Human-centred design starts with the people who'll use what we build. Before any tool, I get to know each person in the team.

  • Who they are and how they work · their role, their habits, the parts of the job they enjoy and the parts that drain them
  • Goals and aspirations · where they want their career and their work to go, and how AI can help them get there
  • Fears and worries · what concerns them about AI, heard openly and taken seriously
  • Trust and relationships · built one conversation and one shared win at a time
  • Culture · how the business works together today, and how that shifts as AI becomes a valued part of the team and its operations

The AI-native design system looks at any business through five core functions. They map how the business operates, from each function down to its workflows and tasks, and give us a way to plan and track the change as it happens.

How the change unfolds

Workflows redesigned

The highest-value workflows rebuilt around what people and AI each do best.

Governance in place

Clear rules for data, approvals and human review, agreed with the directors.

People and AI working together

As trust grows, AI takes on more, and the team spends more time on design and judgement.

3Build it with you

Embedded in your team, building inside the tools you already use, and staying until it's part of everyday work.

Built with you, owned by you

I work alongside the people who do the work and build with them, at their desks. Every AI output has a person reviewing it. Everything we build is documented, and your team owns it. AI changes monthly, so we keep you free to switch tools as better models arrive.

A Company Brain

One organised home for how the business works: processes, decisions, past work, policies and lessons learned. People find what they need in seconds, and AI uses it to draft, check and answer with your knowledge behind it. It grows every time a process is documented or a project finishes.

A shared skills library

When someone works out a great way to use AI, it goes into a team-wide library of skills that everyone can use. Nobody builds alone, and the business gets smarter every week.

Bringing the whole team with you

Tools only matter if people use them. Alongside the building, the team's own confidence grows.

Kick-off session

A short all-hands: what AI can do for a business like yours, live on your own work, and the plan.

Workshops and drop-ins

People bring a real task and leave with it done. Regular drop-ins for help in between.

AI champions

One or two people per team who pick it up fastest, supported to help colleagues and share what works.

Team hackathon

A focused day for the whole company to build something useful, share data and ideas, and see what's possible.

One team, fully AI-native

Start with one team or workflow and redesign it end to end, as the model for everyone else.

Measured from day one

Every workflow tracked against its baseline, so the directors see the value in hours and weeks.

Side-by-side sessions

Two people at one screen, working through a real task together with AI. Each picks up how the other thinks and works, and both finish faster. Habits form when people use AI on their own work, with someone beside them.

The 90 days at a glance

Days 1–30

Discover and quick wins

  • AI Design Sprint with each team
  • Tools, access and data review
  • Simple AI use policy agreed
  • Kick-off session
  • 2–3 quick wins in use
Days 31–60

Build and embed

  • 2–3 deeper workflows built with their owners
  • Side-by-side sessions and drop-ins
  • AI champions named
  • Shared skills library started
  • Measuring against the baseline
Days 61–90

Scale and plan ahead

  • Harden and document what works
  • Team hackathon
  • Results shared with the directors
  • 6–12 month AI roadmap
  • Review: what next, together

What success looks like on day 90

Workflows in daily use, with measured hours and weeks saved
A team that's confident building with AI, with champions in place
Cleaner, more reachable data and clear rules for using AI safely
A 6–12 month roadmap agreed with the directors
